ISSUE #1

Contactless Railway Track Crack Detection System

A small, contactless prototype vehicle that moves along a railway track and uses infrared and ultrasonic sensors to spot a possible crack or gap. When it finds one, it stops at that point, sounds a local alarm and sends an SMS alert through a GSM module saying a crack has been detected.

  • 1Scan the track with IR and ultrasonic sensors
  • 2Stop at the gap and sound the alarm
  • 3Send an SMS alert via the GSM module
